7  Instance pages - Data files

Now that you have made your new training instance, it is time to fill it with the content of your training. In this chapter, you will add all the content that describved your training, in data files, like for the landing page. How to add your training materials and instructor notes follows in the next chapter, Instance pages - Content.

The figure below shows the parts of a training instance website, and the names this guide uses for them.

FIG fig-alt=“Annotated example of a training instance website: the navigation bar with logos, the welcome section, the registration banner, the Next up section, the quick links, announcements, the team preview and the footer.”

In this chapter, you will learn how to:

  • Switch off template mode: replace the README and remove the template banner.
  • Fill in the required information: the training, the team and the overview page.
  • Add the optional pages: the schedule, announcements, practicalities, preparation, FAQ and resources.

Before you start

Work on the branch of your training instance, for example release-2705, as described in Regular workflow.

Everything in this chapter happens in the data files in the data folder. Each data file explains how it works, in the lines starting with #: which fields are required, what they do, and the rules they must follow. Read these explanations carefully before you fill in a file.

7.1 Switch off template mode

Do this once, on the branch of your first training instance: the one you created from release-0000 (see Create your first new training instance). Later additional training instances start from the branch of your first training instance, so all changes you’ve made on that branch will be copied.

Replace the README

Just like for the landing page, your new training instance branch still has the README of the template. Replace it with the ready-made README for a training instance, which is already on your branch, in the docs folder:

  1. Open docs/_template-README.md, select all text, and copy it.
  2. Open README.md, at the top level of your training instance repository. Select all text, and paste, so the copied text replaces it.
  3. Delete the explanation at the top of the file, between <!-- and -->, and the note starting with > **Template:**.
  4. Fill in the parts marked Write this yourself, and save the file.

The other parts of the README, between <!-- generated:... --> lines, fill in by themselves, from the data files of your training instance.

NoteOptional: delete docs/_template-README.md

You now no longer need docs/_template-README.md. In the Explorer panel, right-click the file and choose Delete.

Remove the template banner

The training instance also has a template banner, and it is turned off in the same way as for the landing page.

  1. Open data/website.yml.
  2. Change template: true to template: false.
  3. Save the file.
  4. The yellow Want to use this template? banner disappears from the overview page.

7.2 Required information

Every training instance requires three data files to be filled in: course.yml, team.yml and website.yml.

The training: course.yml

data/course.yml describes your training instance. Most of it appears on the overview page and the Syllabus page.

Open data/course.yml, and work through it from the top down up to (not including) the reuse part. The comments at the top of the data file list which fields are required and which are optional.

Leaving out optional fields. If an optional field doesn’t apply to your training, such as credits or examination, you can leave it out in two ways:

  • delete it: the field name and all lines that belong to it;
  • leave it empty: nothing after the colon, "" or [].
Tip

Use the same title, description, learning outcomes and keywords as on the landing page, unless this training instance is different. The landing page and the training instances then describe the same training.

NoteSelf-paced training
  • Leave out start_date and end_date.
  • Use duration for the expected workload, for example "About 10 hours".
  • Set mode and location to "Online".
  • Describe how participants work in instruction, for example "Self-study".
NoteTraining in cohorts

It depends on how you set up your training instances (see Training instances):

  • One training instance per cohort: fill in the dates of that cohort, as for any other training instance.
  • One training instance for several cohorts: leave out start_date and end_date, and list the cohorts and their dates on the Announcements or Practicalities page instead.

Reuse and citation. The reuse part at the bottom of data/course.yml describes how others may reuse and cite your training. Right now, the only thing to fill in is the licence:

  • licence and licence_url: CC BY 4.0 by default. Keep it, or change it. If you change it, also replace the text in LICENSE.md with the full text of your new licence.

Leave the other fields empty for now. They behave the same as in landing page and will be explained in FAIRification.

Important

Creating a training instance from one of your own earlier training instances? Then version_doi and release_date are probably filled in: empty them, because they belong to the earlier training instance. Keep doi: it stays the same for all your training instances.

The team: team.yml

The people in data/team.yml appear on the Team page, on the Syllabus page, and in a team preview on the overview page. They are also credited in the citation of the training instance.

Open data/team.yml and fill in each person. To add a person, copy a whole entry, from - given_names: to the line before the next person, paste it below, and change the values. To remove a person, delete their whole entry.

Important
  • Every person needs given_names, family_names, roles and affiliation. The roles are Training lead, Instructor and Contributor.
  • At least one person needs course_contact: true and an email: participants contact them with questions.

The overview page shows up to two people: training leads first, then instructors. Photos are optional: put them in the img/photos folder, and add their path under image.

The overview page: website.yml

data/website.yml sets the parts of the overview page that don’t come from other files. Open it, and work through the parts below. You can leave the rest of the file as it is: the content part follows in the next chapter, and the footer fills itself in.

Part of the file What it sets
navbar → logos The logos in the navigation bar. As on the landing page, the logo with primary: true (SciLifeLab) is always on the right, and you set the size of each logo with height. See Logos
welcome The title, text and image of the welcome section. Put the image in the img folder
registration The registration banner: when registration opens and closes, the link to the registration page or form, and the fee
pages The texts on the quick links. You can usually leave them as they are: see the note below
NoteAbout the quick links

The overview page shows up to four quick links: cards that lead to the most important pages. Content and Syllabus always get one. The other pages, in the order Schedule, Practicalities, Preparation, Resources and FAQ, get one only when their data file contains information, while there’s room.

In the pages part of data/website.yml, you can change the title, description and icon of each quick link. The icons come from Bootstrap Icons.

To keep a page out of the quick links, delete its entry in data/website.yml. The page itself stays in the navigation bar, as long as its data file contains information.

NoteAbout the footer

You don’t need to change anything in the footer part of data/website.yml. The footer fills itself in:

  • The organisation (“By …”) comes from organizers in data/course.yml.
  • The licence (“Licensed under …”) comes from licence and licence_url under reuse in data/course.yml.
  • The GitHub logo links to your own repository, worked out automatically.

The built_with part credits the SciLifeLab Training Webpage Template, which your website is built with. Please leave it as it is.

NoteAbout the content part

The content part at the bottom of data/website.yml lists the modules of your training materials, and the order in which they appear in the navigation. Leave it as it is for now: you’ll change it when you add your own materials, as described in Instance pages - Content.

7.3 Optional pages

The other data files are the flexible part of your training instance. Which ones you need depends on your training: a self-paced training has no schedule, an online training needs no practicalities about a venue, and a short workshop may not need announcements.

Page Data file Where it appears
Schedule data/schedule.yml Its own link in the navigation bar, and the Next up section on the overview page
Announcements data/announcements.yml The Information menu, and the two latest announcements on the overview page
Practicalities data/practicalities.yml The Information menu
Preparation data/preparation.yml The Information menu
FAQ data/faq.yml The Information menu
Resources data/resources.yml The Information menu

A page only appears when its data file contains information. Team and Syllabus are always in the Information menu: they come from the required files team.yml and course.yml.

Fill in the files you need, and leave out the others.

Fill in a page

Each data file starts with an explanation of its page. Read it carefully before you fill in the file: it describes what each field does, which fields are required, and how the page behaves. It also has a part called Leaving information out, which explains how to leave out one item or a whole section without breaking the page.

ImportantUsing a schedule? Read this first

The schedule needs a bit more care than the other pages, because the website uses its dates and times:

  • Each event, such as a lecture, an exercise or a break, is one entry, from - title: to content:. Every event needs a title, type, group, start and end.
  • Write dates and times exactly as explained in the file, for example 2027-05-12T09:00, and use one of the event types listed there.
  • Events with the same group, for example "Day 1", are shown together, in order of their start time.
  • To link an event to a page of your materials, fill in content with the path to that page, for example "content/module-1/introduction.qmd".

The Next up section on the overview page then always shows the next event, with a countdown, without you having to change anything during the training.

Leave a page out

Delete its data file, or delete everything in it. The page and its link in the navigation bar disappear by themselves.

Tip

Changed your mind later? Every data file is still in the template, on the release-0000 branch. Copy it from there, or from one of your earlier training instances.

7.4 Cheat sheet

All on the branch of your training instance.

To… Where What to do
Switch off template mode (first instance only) data/website.yml and README.md Set template: false, and replace the README with docs/_template-README.md
Describe the training instance data/course.yml Fill in the required fields listed at the top of the file, and check the licence under reuse
Add the team data/team.yml One entry per person. At least one person with course_contact: true and an email
Set up the overview page data/website.yml logos under navbar, welcome, registration and pages
Fill in an optional page data/schedule.yml, announcements.yml, practicalities.yml, preparation.yml, faq.yml or resources.yml Read the explanation at the top of the file first
Leave out an optional page The page’s data file Delete the file, or everything in it
